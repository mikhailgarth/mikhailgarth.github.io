These essays are musings on how people and events from long ago shaped the world we live in now.  They are for the curious who enjoy the mental expansion that comes from shifting perspective, zooming out from our daily lives to marvel at the panorama of history and the world. It gives us meaning if we can see ourselves as part of a larger picture and realize that men and women have always struggled and had the same desires and motives as we do now.  Distilling human experience enriches us and allows us to cope better with life.
